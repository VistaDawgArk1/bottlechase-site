Where it works

Thousands of liquor stores and distilleries across Alabama, Arkansas, Florida, Georgia, Indiana, Kentucky, Missouri, Mississippi, Tennessee and Texas, with more states on the way. Missing a store? Add it in the app.

Free, and private by design

No ad networks, no tracking and no sign-up form: the app makes an account for you. Stores that pay to be featured are always labeled “Sponsored.” Your exact location stays on your phone. Read the privacy policy.

Bottle Chase is for adults 21 and over. Please drink responsibly, and respect each store’s rules and staff.
